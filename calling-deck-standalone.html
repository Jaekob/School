Calling Deck

Print number cards for cold-calling students. Cut them out, lay each one over a Pokémon card, and slide both into a clear sleeve.

Numbers

Any range from 1 to 100. Colors spread across the rainbow, one shade per group of ten.

Name on cards

Printed in the top right corner of every card. Leave it empty for no name. Saved in this browser.

Card size
×

61 × 86 mm sits just inside a standard 63 × 88 mm Pokémon card, so the paper lies flat in the sleeve.

Color
Options
To print

Press Ctrl + P (⌘ + P on Mac). Choose A4, scale 100% (not "fit to page"), and margins set to none. Turn on "Background graphics" so the colors print.
